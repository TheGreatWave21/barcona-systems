Pilot program

Shape BARCONA with us.

Process

How a pilot runs.

  1. Agree the scope

    We learn about your site and agree on a pilot area and clear success criteria.

  2. Integrate

    We mount Detection Units and connect capture and output to your process.

  3. Measure

    Coverage, inventory time and time saved are measured under real conditions.

  4. Decide

    We compare benefit and effort together. Expansion only follows confirmed results.

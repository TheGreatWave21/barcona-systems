How it works

Four steps to a structured result.

  1. Detect

    Finds handling-unit barcodes, shelf barcodes and the markings that show which shelf level a label belongs to.

  2. Read

    Locates each barcode precisely, prepares the image region and decodes the value.

  3. Assign

    Uses position, markings and shelf level to link every handling unit to its storage location.

  4. Check

    Flags anything uncertain and delivers a clean, structured output. Nothing gets silently guessed.

Transparent results

Uncertainty stays visible.

When the system isn't sure, it says so. Every result carries a clear status, so your team knows exactly what to check.

NO READ

The barcode couldn't be read reliably. It stays in the output as a visible gap rather than disappearing.

REVIEW

A result that needs a human check before it's accepted.

CLASS CONFLICT

It isn't clear what kind of barcode this is, so it's flagged instead of guessed.

2026

Product

From the TraceUnit idea to a software core, and a new Detection Unit

What started as a robot concept with early image recognition has grown into BARCONA AI: our own warehouse dataset and trained models, barcode reading with tracking across video frames, assignment and conflict logic, an interface with Excel output, plus tests and documentation.

With the core in place, we're introducing a second way to deploy it. The Detection Unit mounts a camera and compute module on existing forklifts, so inventory is captured during normal operations. A first test setup is in preparation.

12 Sep 2025

Engineering

Where we stand

A progress update on the TraceUnit. Our focus has been on the core software architecture: the foundation that takes in data from the barcode scanner, camera and LiDAR and turns it into one consistent picture of the warehouse.

With that groundwork in place, the next steps are integrating the sensors on the prototype and testing the full scan cycle.

Aug 2025

Design

The TraceUnit takes shape in CAD

The first complete 3D model of the TraceUnit-1-30x is done, covering the chassis, sensor mast and drive. It's the blueprint for the physical prototype.

Jul 2026

Product

From the TraceUnit idea to a software core, and a new Detection Unit

What started as a robot concept with early image recognition has grown into BARCONA AI, a software core that turns warehouse images and video into inventory data.

With the core in place, we're introducing a second way to deploy it. The Detection Unit mounts a camera and compute module on existing forklifts, so inventory is captured during normal operations. A first test setup is in preparation.

Jun 2026

Engineering

Our own detection model

We've trained a recognition model on images from our own test setups instead of relying on generic models. It's noticeably better at finding barcodes on real warehouse labels, even at an angle or in poor light.

May 2026

Engineering

Testing in simulation first

We built a simulated warehouse hall to test how the robot drives, where it stops and how it scans each rack. Changes can now be tried in minutes before they go anywhere near hardware.

You can watch a simplified version of a scan mission on our homepage.

Apr 2026

Engineering

Camera testing

At the beginning of April we started testing the full camera chain end to end against our new datasets: finding a barcode, moving the camera onto it and reading it reliably.

Later in the month we added a tilt routine: the camera stays on the robot, angles up the rack level by level and only keeps the sharpest frames. That's the scan pattern the TraceUnit will use in the aisle.

Mar 2026

Engineering

Datasets and a moving camera

We've started building our own datasets from real warehouse labels: different printers, angles, distances and lighting. They're what we test and train against from now on.

The camera also learned to pan, tilt and zoom on its own towards what it detects.

Feb 2026

Engineering

The first software is running

The core architecture is in place: the foundation that takes in data from the camera, barcode reading and LiDAR and turns it into one consistent picture of the warehouse.

A first camera setup already picks out barcodes in live images. Next up: data, and lots of it.

Jan 2026

Design

From idea to concept

The idea has become a concrete plan: the TraceUnit, an autonomous robot that drives the aisles, stops at each rack and reads every barcode with its camera, while LiDAR keeps track of where it is.

Sep 2025

Design

Where it started

Stocktakes are slow, manual and out of date the day they finish. That's where Barcona started: with the question of whether a camera could do the counting instead.

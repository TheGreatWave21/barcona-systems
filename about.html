About Barcona

Young team. Real problem. Serious engineering.

Barcona Systems is a student-founded technology startup from Lörrach in Baden-Württemberg. We build autonomous vision systems that help businesses reduce errors, save time and run their inventory more efficiently.

Our story

Why we started.

Inventory is one of those problems everyone accepts as normal: things go missing, counts drift and people spend hours searching. We think automation can fix that.

We started with the idea of a robot that does the tracking itself, the TraceUnit-1-30x. Along the way the idea grew into something bigger: BARCONA AI, a software core that turns camera images and video into reliable inventory data.

Today that core can run on two kinds of hardware: the autonomous TraceUnit, or the Detection Unit, a camera and compute module mounted on the forklifts a warehouse already uses. We design the hardware, write the software and test it ourselves, and we're now looking for partners to take it into real warehouses.

Detection Unit · Live BARCONA AI core

The idea

Your forklifts already pass every shelf.

Instead of sending people, or a robot, to count stock, the Detection Unit records what's on the shelves while forklifts drive their normal routes.

Camera + compute

One compact module per forklift: a camera to capture the shelves and a compute unit running BARCONA AI.

Uses existing movements

Captures data during the work that's happening anyway, with no dedicated scan runs.

Standard components

Built from off-the-shelf parts, so it's quick to mount and easy to roll out across a fleet.

In operation

From shelf to spreadsheet.

  1. Drive

    The forklift works as usual. The module records along the way.

  2. Capture

    Barcodes in the camera's view are captured automatically.

  3. Process

    BARCONA AI turns the images into inventory data.

  4. Compare

    Results are compared with expected stock, and differences are flagged for a targeted check.

Search less. See deviations sooner.

Two ways to deploy

Detection Unit or TraceUnit?

Both run the same BARCONA AI core. The difference is how the camera gets to the shelf.

Detection UnitTraceUnit
ConceptUses existing forklift movementsAutonomous scan missions
SetupCamera + compute unit per forkliftRobot per area

Both are implementation concepts. Driving routes, field of view and mounting are validated in the pilot.

FAQ

Questions.

Does the forklift driver have to do anything?

No. The idea is that capture happens in the background while the forklift does its normal work.

What if a shelf is never passed?

Coverage depends on the driving routes and the camera's field of view. Measuring that is one of the main goals of the pilot. For areas that forklifts rarely visit, the TraceUnit can run defined routes.

When can we test it?

We're aiming for a first Detection Unit test setup within a few weeks. That's a target, not a rollout promise. Apply for the pilot to be part of it.

The problem

Warehouses lose track of what they already own.

Stock gets put in the wrong place, labels get damaged and manual counts lag days behind reality. Each mistake costs time to search, re-pick and correct.

01 / MISPLACED

Wrong shelf, lost item

A package in the wrong location is effectively gone until someone stumbles over it.

02 / MANUAL

Counting takes people

Hand scanners and stocktakes tie up staff and only give you a snapshot, never the current state.

03 / BLIND SPOTS

Labels fail

Damaged, covered or missing barcodes break systems that rely on scanning alone.

BARCONA AI

One image in. A structured result out.

Our software core turns camera images and video into barcode values, location assignments and clear status flags, ready for Excel or your systems.

How it works

Three sensors. One picture of your floor.

Each sensor covers for the weaknesses of the others. Together they give a reliable, continuously updated picture of every package. The animation shows the sensors our robot would incorporate, step by step.

Sensor: Barcode Illustration

Illustration, not a product design. It shows the sensors our robot would incorporate and what the system can do with them as a whole. In practice they sit together on one device; which sensors are used and how they are mounted can be adapted and discussed for your site.

01 · Scan

Reads every label it passes

A camera reads the barcode on every label as the unit drives along the aisle. Nobody has to point a hand scanner.

  • Continuous scanning while moving
  • Identifies item and package ID

02 · Recognise

Sees what labels can't tell it

A camera with AI visual recognition checks what a package actually looks like, so damaged or hidden labels don't leave gaps.

  • Visual cross-check of scanned items
  • Works when labels are unreadable

03 · Map

Knows exactly where everything is

LiDAR maps the space around the unit, giving every detected package a precise position on a live floor map.

  • 360° detection range
  • Real-time location mapping

04 · Flag

Spots mistakes immediately

When something is in the wrong place or labelled incorrectly, the system flags it, so your team fixes it now and not at the next stocktake.

  • Misplaced package detection
  • Mislabel detection

    Progress

    Building in public.

    We're a young team and we share where we are, honestly. Follow along as BARCONA moves from first idea to the warehouse floor.

    Read the updates →
    1. Sep 2025

      The first idea

      Stocktakes are slow, manual and out of date the day they finish. We started asking how a camera could do the counting instead.

    2. Jan 2026

      A concrete concept

      The idea became a plan: the TraceUnit, an autonomous robot with camera, barcode reading and LiDAR that scans racks on its own.

    3. Feb 2026

      The first software

      The core architecture for sensor input, data processing and mapping, plus a first camera setup that picks out barcodes in live images.

    4. Mar 2026

      Datasets & camera control

      We started building our own datasets of real warehouse labels, and the camera learned to pan, tilt and zoom towards what it detects.

    5. Apr 2026

      Camera testing

      From early April we tested the camera chain against our datasets end to end, then added a tilt scan that reads a rack level by level.

    6. May 2026

      Warehouse simulation

      A simulated hall lets us test navigation and scan routines before they run on hardware.

    7. Jun 2026

      Our own detection model

      We trained a recognition model on our own warehouse images.

    8. Jul 2026

      BARCONA AI software core

      The software core is done: it turns warehouse images into barcode values, shelf locations and status flags.

    9. Next

      Detection Unit test setup

      A first forklift-mounted test setup, followed by tests in real warehouse conditions.

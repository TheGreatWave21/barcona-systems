Capabilities

What it does.

Five capabilities, designed to work together on one machine.

Triple-sensor fusion

Barcode scanning, AI visual recognition and LiDAR combined, so one sensor's blind spot is covered by another.

Real-time location mapping

Every detected package appears with its position on a live floor map.

360° detection range

Full awareness around the unit, not just in front of it.

Plug-and-play setup

Designed to start working in an existing warehouse without rebuilding infrastructure.

AI error detection

Automatically flags packages that are misplaced or mislabelled.

Fully autonomous

Drives its own route through the aisles, so your team can focus on the work that needs people.

A scan cycle

From aisle to answer.

  1. Drive

    The unit follows its route through the aisles on its own.

  2. Sense

    Barcode, camera and LiDAR capture data at the same time.

  3. Fuse

    The software combines the readings into one identified, located package.

  4. Report

    The live map updates, and any errors are flagged to your team.

FAQ

Questions.

Can I buy a TraceUnit today?

Not yet. The TraceUnit-1-30x is a prototype. We're working with pilot partners to test it under real conditions before any commercial release.

Does it replace my warehouse management system?

No. The TraceUnit is meant to feed accurate, real-time location data into the processes you already have.

What if a barcode is damaged or missing?

That's what the AI camera is for. Visual recognition and LiDAR positioning help identify and locate packages even when the label alone isn't enough.

How does the TraceUnit compare to the Detection Unit?

Both run the same BARCONA AI core. The TraceUnit drives its own defined routes, independent of forklifts. The Detection Unit mounts on existing forklifts and needs less robotics. See the comparison.
